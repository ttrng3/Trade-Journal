# 15:50 flat

v7 closes anything still open at 15:50 rather than carrying it into the close. The rule removes overnight and
last-minutes risk, and the cost it can impose is cutting a trade that is still moving the right way.

## Evidence
- 2026-10-29, **the manual book, not v7**: nothing enforced a flat time on Ty's own trading and it cost
  **-$605.00**. Two strikes were still open at the bell after buy fills at 15:26:06, 15:36:02, 15:45:32 and
  **16:01:36** — the last after the close and the latest fill in the compiled set — and the five contracts left on
  were sold into Friday's open for **-$371.00** and **-$234.00**. The same session's three same-day strikes, all
  closed by 11:07, were **all green**. This is evidence about the rule's subject rather than about v7's
  implementation of it: on this day the book without the rule lost exactly what the rule exists to prevent.
  See [[mistakes/overnight-0-1-dte]]. (Verified: raw/2026-10-29.json, raw/2026-10-30.json; carry computed over
  data/fills/2026-10.json)
- 2026-10-07: v7-4, META put, closed at 15:50:18 for pnl -$23.30 at r +0.19. The underlying had moved 0.545 the
  trade's way against a 2.8414 stop, so the loss was premium rather than direction. In the 3 candles between the exit
  and the close META fell a further 0.53 to 721.15, meaning the rule gave up roughly as much again as the trade had
  earned. t1 at 719.6436 was never reached either way. (Verified: raw/2026-10-07.json)
- 2026-10-07: the drill v7-3 also closed on this rule at 15:50:16, pnl +$3.70 at r -0.24. Drills are not counted.
  (Verified: raw/2026-10-07.json)

## Count so far
Seen on 2 of 18 compiled days. On v7 it has fired once on a live trade, 2026-10-07, giving up a further **0.53** of
favourable underlying movement, about **0.19R** on that trade's stop distance. On the manual book the rule does not
exist, and the one session where it would have bound — 2026-10-29 — carried **5** contracts past the bell for
**-$605.00**. The two readings point opposite ways and neither is a sample: the v7 instance cost a small amount of
upside, the manual instance cost the largest carry compiled.
(Verified: computed over raw/2026-10-07…raw/2026-10-30; carry computed over data/fills/2026-10.json)

## What to do about it
Proposals only, for the Friday tune. One instance is not a case for moving the time. The number worth watching is how
much favourable movement the rule gives up per day against the risk it removes; if the give-up stays this consistent
across a week, a trailing stop into the close is worth costing out as an alternative to a hard flat time. Note also
that v7-4 was an entry at 15:09:25 with a 40-minute window before a known flat time, which is the other half of the
question.
