# 15:50 flat

v7 closes anything still open at 15:50 rather than carrying it into the close. The rule removes overnight and
last-minutes risk, and the cost it can impose is cutting a trade that is still moving the right way.

## Evidence
- 2026-10-07: v7-4, META put, closed at 15:50:18 for pnl -$23.30 at r +0.19. The underlying had moved 0.545 the
  trade's way against a 2.8414 stop, so the loss was premium rather than direction. In the 3 candles between the exit
  and the close META fell a further 0.53 to 721.15, meaning the rule gave up roughly as much again as the trade had
  earned. t1 at 719.6436 was never reached either way. (Verified: raw/2026-10-07.json)
- 2026-10-07: the drill v7-3 also closed on this rule at 15:50:16, pnl +$3.70 at r -0.24. Drills are not counted.
  (Verified: raw/2026-10-07.json)

## Count so far
Seen on 1 of 1 compiled days, once on a live trade. The one live instance gave up a further 0.53 of favourable
underlying movement, about 0.19R on that trade's stop distance.
(Verified: computed over raw/2026-10-07…2026-10-07)

## What to do about it
Proposals only, for the Friday tune. One instance is not a case for moving the time. The number worth watching is how
much favourable movement the rule gives up per day against the risk it removes; if the give-up stays this consistent
across a week, a trailing stop into the close is worth costing out as an alternative to a hard flat time. Note also
that v7-4 was an entry at 15:09:25 with a 40-minute window before a known flat time, which is the other half of the
question.
