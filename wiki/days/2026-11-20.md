## Lessons carried to the next session
- **The dump is a bad habit, not a bad exit, and this session separates the two for the first time.** Eight
  instances, **33** contracts, **-$1,888.00**, all eight red — and all eight right, because the underlying was
  lower at the close in every case. The cost was paid building the ladder downward into a falling tape, not
  taking it off. The page's running record is now **18** instances and **18** red, but the two sessions
  underneath that number are opposite in kind: on 2026-11-13 the dumps gave away a strike that settled **$6.99 in
  the money**, and here they gave away nothing at all. The usable rule is about the adds, not the sell.
  See [[mistakes/dump-below-the-ladder]].
  (Verified: raw/2026-11-20.json; computed over raw/2026-11-10…raw/2026-11-20)

  of open cost sit at 5 DTE or longer. On the session's cash the same split reads **+$194.00** same-day against
